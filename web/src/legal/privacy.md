# Privacy notice

**Draft for the invite-only beta, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.**

Version 2026-10-13-draft. This notice explains what this service keeps about you, why, who it is shared with, and how you control it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

## Who runs this service

[Operator name], [city], India, is responsible for your personal data here (the "Data Fiduciary"). Questions, requests and complaints: [contact email]. We answer within 30 days.

## What we keep, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address, from signing in | To sign you in and to contact you about your account |
| The text of the resumes you add (not the files themselves), of your portfolio page and public GitHub repositories if you add them, and any note you write | To draft your profile facts for you to confirm |
| Your LinkedIn, GitHub and portfolio links, if you give them | To put them on the resumes built for you |
| The facts, skills and preferences you confirm | To find and rank jobs for you and to build your resumes |
| The jobs ranked for you, your decisions on them, and the resumes built | To show you your search and where each application stands |
| Your "good match?" answers | To check and improve how well jobs are ranked |
| The questions setup asks about your work, and your answers | To complete your profile facts - only what you confirm is used |
| How many AI requests were made for you, and what they cost | To keep each person within their limit |
| The day you last used the service (the day only) | To count how many people use it each week |

We keep nothing else. Details a resume often carries that we don't need - date of birth, Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers, family members' names, marital status, religion, home address and references - are removed as the file is read: we never keep them or send them anywhere. We never read your LinkedIn page; we only keep its address. We do not read your email inbox, we do not use your data for advertising, and we never sell it.

## AI providers

Some steps send text to an AI model run by another company:

- **Drafting your profile:** the text of your resumes, portfolio page, GitHub repositories and note, with your email, phone number, links and the personal details above removed, and your answers to setup's questions about your work, go to Anthropic (Claude). Setup never asks about your age, family, religion, caste, gender, health, salary or notice period.
- **Ranking jobs and writing a resume's summary line:** your job history (titles, employers, dates), education, confirmed facts and skills go to Anthropic (Claude) and Groq, without your name or contact details.

These providers process the text only to answer the request; their terms for this kind of use say they do not train their models on it.

## Where your data is kept

- The database and sign-in: Supabase, in its Mumbai, India region.
- The application server: Render, in Singapore.
- AI providers: Anthropic and Groq, in the United States.

So some of your data is processed outside India, in countries the law allows transfers to.

## How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything kept for you is deleted at once from the live service, and from backups within 30 days. Records the law requires us to keep (for example, of a data breach) are kept only as long as it requires.

## Your rights

- **See it:** Settings > Download everything gives you all we keep about you.
- **Correct it:** change your facts and preferences in Setup at any time.
- **Erase it, and withdraw consent:** Settings > Delete my account. Withdrawing consent means we stop processing and delete your data.
- **Nominate someone** to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to: email [contact email].
- **Complain:** email [contact email] first. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Who can use this service

You must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly keep data about children.

## Security and breaches

Your data is reached only after you sign in, travels encrypted, and is separated from everyone else's. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires: what happened, what it means for you, and what we are doing about it.

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will show you the new version and ask for your consent again before continuing.
